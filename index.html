COPY, PASTE, GO

50 AI prompts most beginner guides don't give you

Every prompt is written to work in ChatGPT, Claude, or any capable AI assistant. Swap out the {brackets}, paste it in, and you have a first draft in seconds — not a blank page.

Idea Brainstorm
Act as a business strategist. I know a lot about {topic or skill}. List 10 problems people in that world complain about often...
Write a Sample Deliverable
Act as a professional {service type}. Create a finished sample of {deliverable} for a {type of business}, ready to send to a real client...
Handle a Price Objection
A potential client said: "{objection}." Help me write three different responses: one reassuring, one lower-commitment...
Ask for a Testimonial
Write a short, friendly message asking a happy customer for a testimonial, making it easy for them by suggesting what to mention...
